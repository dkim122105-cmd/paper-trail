Paper Trail

The true stories behind money, tech and AI.

Paper Trail is a YouTube channel of short documentary videos. Each one follows the paper trail behind a famous money, tech or AI story, and every claim on air is checked against sources before the video is made.

Watch on YouTube: @followpapertrailhq

How the videos are made

The narration voice and some visuals are generated with AI tools, and every upload is labelled as containing synthetic content. Historical images are public-domain archival material, credited in each video's description.

The uploader

The channel owner publishes videos with a small private tool that uses YouTube API Services. It is not a public app. See the privacy policy.
