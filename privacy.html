Privacy policy

Paper Trail uploader · effective 29 September 2026

What this covers

The "Paper Trail uploader" (listed in Google as story-engine uploader) is a private command-line tool. The owner of the Paper Trail YouTube channel uses it to upload videos to that one channel. It has one user, the channel owner, and it is not offered to anyone else.

YouTube API Services

The uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

It requests one permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the signed-in account's channel. It does not read, collect, or store any other YouTube or Google data, and it never accesses anyone else's account.

How that data is stored and shared

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's, and it is never shared with or sold to anyone. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Deleting it

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it, or deleting the local token file, removes the uploader's access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this site's repository.
